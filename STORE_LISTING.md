## Full description

```
Karakeep Reader lets you read the articles you've saved in your own Karakeep
(formerly Hoarder) server straight from your wrist.

FEATURES
• Browse your latest bookmarks: unarchived, favourites or all
• Read the full article text on your watch, with smooth scrolling
• Uses the content Karakeep has already crawled, so articles load fast
• Works with text notes as well as saved links
• Text size follows your watch's system setting, or choose your own size
  separately for the list and for the article
• Light and dark themes
• Long-press Select in the list to refresh

SETUP
1. In Karakeep, go to Settings > API Keys and create a new key.
2. Open Karakeep Reader's settings in the Pebble app on your phone.
3. Enter your Karakeep server URL (e.g. https://karakeep.example.com)
   and your API key.

NOTES
• Requires your own Karakeep instance (self-hosted or hosted) and an internet
  connection on your phone. Your server URL and API key are stored only in
  the Pebble app on your phone, and are used only to talk to your server.
• Text only: images in articles are not shown, and very long articles are
  cut off after about 14,000 characters.
• Built for Pebble Time 2 (emery), Pebble Round 2 (gabbro), Pebble 2 Duo
  (flint) and Pebble 2 (diorite).

Karakeep Reader is an unofficial, community app and is not affiliated with
Karakeep.
```
